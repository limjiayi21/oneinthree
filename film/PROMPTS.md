# Part 1 film: image and clip prompts

Process: generate stills first, then animate them with image-to-video.
Each image prompt is sent as `<image prompt> <style suffix>`. Each motion prompt
is sent as `<motion prompt> <style suffix>`. Wherever the woman appears, the
character description below is part of the prompt.

## Style suffix (every image and clip)

> Hand-drawn 2D animation in a textured oil pastel and chalk style, visible
> crayon strokes and fine speckled grain over everything, flat simple shapes,
> limited palette of mustard yellow, deep ultramarine blue, black, warm grey
> and muted teal, with one coral red accent, large areas of solid black, soft
> simplified faces with minimal detail, quiet and melancholic mood, no text,
> no logos.

## Characters

- **The woman:** a young woman with black hair tied in a bun, wearing a coral red coat.
- **The man:** only ever shown in deep blue and black.

## Clip A: the walk

- **Image:** The woman in the coral red coat walking alone down a city street at
  night, seen from behind, mustard yellow streetlight glow, scaffolding drawn in
  black lines, wet pavement with blue reflections, deep black sky.
- **Motion:** She walks slowly away from the camera; the camera follows gently;
  the pastel grain shimmers.

## Clip B: the keys

- **Image:** Close-up of a hand in a coral red sleeve gripping keys with one key
  pushed out between the fingers, against a black background with streaks of
  ultramarine blue.
- **Motion:** The hand tightens its grip; very slow push in; grain shimmers.

## Clip C: the shadow

- **Image:** A woman's long shadow stretching across mustard-lit pavement, and a
  second, larger shadow in deep blue behind it.
- **Motion:** The blue shadow slowly grows closer to hers; the light flickers
  slightly.

## Clip D: the empty street

- **Image:** An empty street at night, black buildings with a single lit window in
  mustard yellow, fine grain falling like light rain.
- **Motion:** Static shot; only the rain-like grain falls and the window light
  flickers softly.

## Clip E: from across the road

- **Image:** Wide side view of a night street from a high angle. A broad dark
  road runs across the middle of the frame, with painted lane lines. On the far
  pavement, the small figure of the woman in the coral red coat walks under a
  mustard streetlight, seen from the side. On the near pavement in the
  foreground, separated from her by the whole road, a warm-grey figure stands
  still, facing across the road toward her, seen from behind and slightly to
  the side. The two are clearly on opposite sides of the street.
- **Motion:** Very slow push in past the foreground figure toward her.

Earlier image prompt for E (images E-1 to E-3; it put both figures on the same
pavement): "Wide view across a night street: in the far distance, the small
figure of the woman in coral red under the streetlights; in the foreground,
the dark warm-grey silhouette of a person standing on this side of the street,
watching."
